VainSabers motion blur fixes for Beat Saber 1.40.8

Hi, I put together a patch for the motion blur based on VainSabers commit 6f85587.

The old blur stepped between recorded poses, and the shader could make a sideways swing look almost solid. The new shader interpolates between adjacent poses and fades the sides and tip. It uses eight poses instead of sixteen and runs in the existing saber rendering passes.

A swing toward the camera could make the blade almost disappear in the middle. The tube shader now measures motion across the blade’s projected width. When that motion is near zero, it keeps the blade close to its current pose and preserves its base opacity.

At high speeds, the history window could include both directions of a reversal, creating a second, sharp wedge. The fix uses the latest continuous segment, caps extreme sweep distance, and resamples that segment into the existing pose buffer. The default blade ribbon also fades in across the overlap window in both its color and glow passes.

OBJ vertices now use both position and rotation from each sampled pose. The shader measures the translation and angular motion visible on screen, then feathers the resulting sweep along both visible axes. Shared positions use the same sample age at normal and UV seams.

I verified stationary, sideways, head-on, fast, and reversing swings in Unity with Direct3D 11. The head-on center matched the stationary blade in the controlled render, and the duplicate wedge disappeared on reversal. A test OBJ stayed sharp at rest and in depth-only motion, then developed soft edges during both lateral translation and rotation. The Release build targets Beat Saber 1.40.8. In-headset behavior, VR frame timing, and a direct ReeSabers comparison still need testing.

The changes are in BlurPart.cginc, BlurSaberPart.cs, BlurMotionHistory.cs, and the blade ribbon code and shader. After applying the changes, rebuild vs_assets with BuildAssetBundles.bat, then rebuild the mod to embed the shaders in VainSabers.dll.
